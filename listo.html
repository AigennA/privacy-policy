Privacy Policy

Listo — Android shopping list app
Last updated: 8 September 2026

Listo is a shopping list app that runs entirely on your device. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: your lists never leave your phone, and we do not collect or receive any personal data about you.

1. Data you create in the app

Your shopping lists, items, prices, budgets, categories, dates, reminders and app settings are stored only in local storage on your device. There is no account, no sign-up and no server component. The developer has no access to this data.

Uninstalling Listo deletes this data. Because it is stored only on your device, we cannot restore it for you. If your device backup is enabled, Android may include this data in your own Google backup — that backup is controlled by you and Google, not by Listo.

2. Advertising

Listo is free and displays a single banner advertisement supplied by Google AdMob. To serve those ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type, operating system version and general interaction with the ad.

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent controller. It is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Listo asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change that choice at any time from the app's Settings screen.

You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

3. Permissions Listo asks for

  • Notifications — used only to show the reminders you set yourself. Declining it does not affect any other feature.
  • Internet — used only to load the banner advertisement.
  • Run at startup — used only to re-arm your reminders after the device restarts.

4. Analytics and tracking

Listo contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no tracking of your behaviour inside the app. Nothing about how you use Listo is sent to the developer.

5. Children

Listo is a general-audience utility app. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Your rights

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request. Data held by Google in connection with advertising is governed by Google's own policy, linked above.
